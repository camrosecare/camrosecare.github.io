## 3. Developmental Programs (Ages 0 to 12 Years)

### 1. Infant Nurture Program (0 – 18 Months)
* **Educator Ratio:** 1:3 to 1:4 Ratio
* **Schedule:** Full-time & part-time slots
* **Curriculum Focus:** Responsive caregiving, individual biological sleep rhythms, sensory exploration, and tummy time on soft textured mats.
* **Highlights:** Quiet dedicated sleep room, daily digital sleep & feeding log, continuous cuddle time.

### 2. Toddler Discovery Studio (19 Months – 3 Years)
* **Educator Ratio:** 1:6 Ratio
* **Schedule:** Full-time & part-time slots
* **Curriculum Focus:** Gross and fine motor development, language explosion, peer social-emotional learning, and toilet-learning collaboration.
* **Highlights:** Natural loose-parts play, indoor active movement zone, music circles, sensory water tables.

### 3. Early Learning & Preschool (3 – 4.5 Years)
* **Educator Ratio:** 1:8 Ratio
* **Schedule:** Full-time & half-day preschool
* **Curriculum Focus:** Play-based foundational math, pre-literacy phonics, Reggio and Montessori-inspired project inquiry, and cooperative problem-solving.
* **Highlights:** Science discovery laboratory, story workshops, dramatic play centers, conflict resolution guidance.

### 4. Kindergarten & Out-of-School Care (4.5 – 12 Years)
* **Educator Ratio:** 1:10 to 1:15 Ratio
* **Schedule:** Before & after school care, PD Days & summer camps
* **Curriculum Focus:** Nutritious after-school snacks, homework assistance lounge, STEM maker projects, outdoor athletic games, and board game leagues.
* **Transportation:** Own daycare-operated school bus offered as an add-on for $50/month between all schools inside Camrose and the daycare.

## 5. Parent Resources & Government Grants

1. **Alberta Child Care Affordability Grant:** Universal fee reduction applied directly to monthly invoices for all children under kindergarten age.
2. **Alberta Provincial Child Care Subsidy:** Additional income-tested subsidy for qualifying households (up to $180,000 gross annual income).
3. **First-Day Packing Checklist:**
   * 2 complete sets of season-appropriate change clothes (labelled)
   * Clean indoor velcro running shoes
   * Cuddly comfort blanket or toy for rest time
   * Outdoor weather gear (sun hat in summer, snowsuit & mittens in winter)
   * Refillable spill-proof water bottle
   * Updated immunization record on file
4. **Health & Illness Exclusion Policy:**
   * Children running a fever of 38°C (100.4°F) or displaying contagious symptoms must remain symptom-free for 24–48 hours before returning.

## 6. How to Run the Exported React + Vite Project

* **Export Archive:** `camrose_care_concept_a_audit_package.zip` (generated directly from live source files).

* **Production Build:** Run `npm run build` to output the production bundle into `dist/`, then `npm run preview` to verify locally.
